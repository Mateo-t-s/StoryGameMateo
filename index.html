Welcome to Escape From Ms. Fong's Classroom After the Bell Rings

The math ain't mathing, can you escape?

You are in the midst of a strenuous Fong quiz when the bell rings, signaling lunchtime. Sadly, Ms. Fong is not required to obey the rules of the school, and will keep you in class until you finish the quiz.

You are supposed to go play craps in the Burger King across the street and need a way to escape before it's too late.

Time is ticking. You need to act quickly without being noticed. Good luck!
